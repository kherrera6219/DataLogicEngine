# Phase 12 Deferred Release Gates

Date: 2026-07-14

- CP12-C: rebuilt-installed Electron workflows must prove real API/IPC and
  durable PostgreSQL, Redis, Neo4j, Chroma, and object-store effects.
- CP12-E: packaged zoom, Windows display/text scaling, high contrast, reduced
  motion, long-content, and screenshot checks remain open.
- CP12-F: manual NVDA acceptance remains open.
- Storage restore remains an offline coordinated recovery workflow and must be
  qualified in the rebuilt application rather than exposed as an unsafe live
  renderer mutation.
- Support bundle owner UI/preview/export is a Phase 13 dependency and remains
  open; only the redacted command-line foundation exists today.
- Earlier installed Phase 3-11 gates, alert 389, Replacement Control,
  independent reviews, signing, and final release acceptance remain unchanged.
