1. **Domain (Pillar)**: 1=Life Sciences, 2=Technology, 3=Business, 4=Regulations, 5=Social, 6=Physical Sciences
2. **Sector**: Industry-specific classification (use NAICS-style codes 1-99)
3. **Honeycomb**: Cross-domain context (1-20 for interconnection strength)
4. **Branch**: Sub-hierarchy within domain (1-10 levels)
5. **Node**: Granular item identifier (1-999)
6. **Octopus**: Central hub concept strength (1-10)
7. **Spiderweb**: Regulatory link density (1-10)
8. **Knowledge Role**: Expert type (1=Knowledge, 2=Industry, 3=Regulatory, 4=Compliance)
9. **Industry Context**: Sector-specific weight (1-10)
10. **Regulatory Context**: Compliance importance (1-10)
11. **Compliance Context**: Cross-regulation impact (1-10)
12. **Location**: Geographic relevance (1-50 for regions)
13. **Temporal**: Time sensitivity (1=Historical, 5=Current, 9=Future-critical)
