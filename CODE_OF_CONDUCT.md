## Our Commitment

We as members, contributors, and leaders of the DataLogicEngine community are committed to making participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to acting and interacting in ways that promote an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and professionally healthy community.

---

## Community Standards

### Expected Behaviors

The following behaviors are expected of all community members and will be enforced:

- **Professionalism:** Engage with colleagues, contributors, and maintainers in a respectful, professional manner.
- **Empathy and Inclusion:** Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward others. Acknowledge and respect differing perspectives, experiences, and backgrounds.
- **Constructive Discourse:** Give and receive feedback constructively. Critique ideas, not individuals. Focus on the technical merits of contributions.
- **Accountability:** Accept responsibility for mistakes, offer sincere apologies when appropriate, and learn from the experience without defensiveness.
- **Community Focus:** Prioritize actions that benefit the project and community as a whole, not just individual interests.
- **Good Faith Collaboration:** Assume good intent in others' contributions and communications unless clear evidence indicates otherwise.

### Unacceptable Behaviors

The following behaviors are prohibited and subject to enforcement action:

- **Sexual harassment:** Use of sexualized language, imagery, or advances in any form.
- **Personal attacks:** Trolling, insulting, demeaning, or derogatory comments directed at individuals or groups.
- **Political or religious attacks:** Personal attacks based on political affiliation, religion, or belief system.
- **Harassment:** Public or private harassment of any kind, including repeated unwanted contact.
- **Doxxing:** Publishing others' private information (physical address, email, phone number, employer, etc.) without their explicit written consent.
- **Intimidation:** Threatening behavior, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussions, events, or collaborative work.
- **Discrimination:** Conduct that demeans or excludes individuals based on any characteristic listed in [Our Commitment](#our-commitment).
- **Professional misconduct:** Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional enterprise setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces operated by or affiliated with DataLogicEngine, including:

- GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, commit messages)
- Official communication channels (email, chat platforms, mailing lists)
- Official events, conferences, meetups (online or in-person)
- Social media accounts maintained by the project

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the DataLogicEngine project in public spaces, such as:

- Using an official project email address
- Posting from an official project social media account
- Serving as a designated representative at a public event

Private conduct outside these spaces is generally outside the scope of this policy, except where it creates a pattern of behavior that affects community safety.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clearly communicating and consistently enforcing the standards defined in this document.
- Taking prompt, fair, and proportionate corrective action in response to reported violations.
- Communicating the reasons for moderation decisions to affected parties where appropriate.
- Removing, editing, or declining content (comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, etc.) that violates this Code of Conduct.
- Maintaining confidentiality of reporters to the extent permitted by law.

## Reporting Violations

### How to Report

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it promptly.

**Primary Reporting Channel:**

Email: [conduct@datalogicengine.com](mailto:conduct@datalogicengine.com)

**GitHub Private Report:**

For incidents occurring within GitHub, you may also use GitHub's built-in reporting tools to flag content directly.

### What to Include in a Report

To enable timely and thorough investigation, include:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | A clear description of the incident |
| **Date and time** | When the incident occurred |
| **Location** | Where it occurred (GitHub issue URL, chat channel, event, etc.) |
| **Parties involved** | Usernames or names of individuals involved |
| **Evidence** | Screenshots, links, or other supporting material |
| **Impact** | How the behavior affected you or others |
| **Desired outcome** | Optional — what resolution you are seeking |

### Confidentiality

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality. Reporter identity will not be disclosed to the subject of the report without the reporter's explicit consent, except where legally required.

### Response Timeline

| Stage | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| Acknowledgement of receipt | Within 48 hours |
| Initial assessment | Within 5 business days |
| Resolution or escalation | Dependent on severity and complexity |

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will apply the following graduated enforcement framework, calibrated to the nature and severity of the violation.

### Level 1: Private Warning

**Trigger:** Minor violation — use of inappropriate language, a single instance of unprofessional communication, or other low-impact conduct.

**Action:**
- A private, written warning from a community leader.
- Explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate and what is expected going forward.
- A public apology may be requested if the incident occurred in a public forum.

### Level 2: Formal Warning

**Trigger:** A more significant single incident, a pattern of minor violations, or conduct that disrupts community participation.

**Action:**
- A formal written warning with documented consequences for repeated behavior.
- Mandatory temporary disengagement from the community for a defined period (typically 7–30 days).
- Prohibition on unsolicited interaction with affected parties and enforcement team members during this period, across all project spaces and external channels.
- Violation of these terms escalates to Level 3.

### Level 3: Temporary Suspension

**Trigger:** Sustained inappropriate conduct, a serious single violation, or violation of a Level 2 warning.

**Action:**
- Temporary suspension from all community participation and public communication for a defined period (typically 30–90 days).
- No public or private interaction with affected parties or enforcement team members during the suspension period.
- Access to project repositories and communication channels may be restricted.
- Violation of these terms results in permanent ban.

### Level 4: Permanent Ban

**Trigger:** A demonstrated pattern of violating community standards, severe harassment, sustained targeted aggression, or egregious misconduct.

**Action:**
- Permanent removal from all project spaces and community participation.
- No appeal pathway for permanent bans unless new material evidence is presented within 90 days.
